- **PoB (checked):** PoB counts the socketed spells at their full self-cast rate.
- **Now:** Custom model (models.energy_model) for Cast on Critical, Cast on Elemental Ailment, Elemental Invocation; Cast on Block (Svalinn; 25 Energy per block of the boss's hits that land), Thundergod's Wrath (Mjolner; 3 per Power per melee hit), Spellslinger (Dueling Wand; 0.2 per 10 ms of the cast spell's base cast time), Barrier Invocation (gem, and on Lament Amulet; 1 Energy per 10 Energy Shield the boss's hits take after mitigation), and (7 Nov) Cast on Dodge: 2 Energy per metre (its in-game text) over the dodge roll's base 4.6 m (dodge_roll_base_travel_distance 46 units, game files; the user had heard 3.7 m), at the user's default of one roll per 2 s against a boss; a build counting on it loses the roll time (0.75 s per roll, DodgeRollPlayer's cast time) from its main skill as less Attack and Cast Speed; both said in the guide.
